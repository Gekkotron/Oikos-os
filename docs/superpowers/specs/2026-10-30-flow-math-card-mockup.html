3 · How do you want to pick functions?

Click the one you prefer (you can say “several” in the terminal).

A

“ƒ Functions” menu

A button under the formula opens a list, each line with what it does and an example. Tapping one inserts it at the cursor with placeholders, e.g. clamp(▢, ▢, ▢).

clamp(x, low, high)keep x between low and high
interp(x, [..], [..])lookup table, linear
if(cond, a, b)a when cond, else b
round(x, n)n decimals
… min · max · abs · floor · ceil
B

Suggestions while typing

Type two letters and matching functions and input names pop up under the field; Tab or tap inserts. No separate button.

heater - cl
clamp(x, low, high)keep x between low and high
ceil(x)round up
C

Both

The ƒ Functions menu for browsing, plus suggestions while typing for speed. Most work, most comfortable on a phone and a desktop.
